Research at a glance

Flat fastballs miss bats up, not down

Among 2026 four-seamers, high-zone whiff rate climbs from 20% for the steepest fifth (location-adjusted VAA) to 35% for the flattest. In the bottom third of the zone the gap almost vanishes (11% vs 14%).

VAA analysis →

Tunnels buy chases, not whiffs

When a breaking ball follows a fastball, a tighter "tunnel" (the two pitches closer together 23.8 ft from the plate) goes with much higher chase rates: 51% under 2 inches vs 24% at 16–18 inches. Whiff rate per swing doesn't follow the same pattern.

Tunneling →

A slider fixed by location, not shape

Noah Cameron buried 57% of his sliders below the zone from July on, up from 37%. Its expected run value per 100 went from −2.3 to +0.1 with the same shape.
