Reports

The daily report & pitcher cards

This is the part of the job I'd do every morning. make report refreshes the data, rebuilds the DuckDB tables with SQL, and renders R Markdown reports whose tables come straight from SQL chunks. Headless Chrome then prints them to one-page PDFs. Below: the report for the Royals' last 2026 game, plus a printable card for every pitcher with 300+ pitches.

Daily pitching report

For each pitcher who threw: velocity, ride, run, spin and release vs his own season baseline, with automatic flags (velocity down 1.5+ mph, shape change of 2+ inches, release drift of 4+ inches). It also covers workload (rest days, pitches over the last 7 days) and an attack / avoid / put-away sheet for each opposing hitter, using shrunk splits by pitch group.

Pitcher card / game plan

One page per pitcher: arsenal with Stuff+ and VAA, movement profile, pitch mix by count, where his best pitch lands by batter side, fastball velocity by pitch count, and three auto-generated game-plan bullets. Every sentence is filled in from the data, so it updates daily without anyone rewriting it.
