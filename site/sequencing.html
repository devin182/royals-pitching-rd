Method

  • Unit: every pitch after the first in a plate appearance (same pitcher), 2026 regular season, league-wide. Pitch types are grouped: CU covers curveballs, knuckle curves, slurves and slow curves, and FS includes forkballs.
  • Both models use xgboost with identical settings. The sequence model adds the previous pitch's type, location (5 zones) and result, plus a same-type flag. Location of the next pitch is left out on purpose, because the question is about choosing a pitch type, not about how well it is located.
  • Validation: train on games through , test on later games. Lift is the % reduction in held-out log loss (MSE for xRV), with a 95% interval from resampling games.
  • Royals leads compare actual results with the sequence model's expectation, after removing each pitcher's average over- or under-performance on that pitch type, so that a pitcher's overall quality doesn't show up as a "sequencing effect". Code: R/06_sequencing.R.
