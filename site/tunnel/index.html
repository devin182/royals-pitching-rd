Each path is that pitch type's average Statcast trajectory, not a single pitch. Flying the averaged 9 parameters to the plate puts the path about 1 inch higher than the average observed plate height (mean |Δ| 0.98 in vertical, 0.25 in horizontal across 120 pitch types), so read separations to the nearest inch.

Method. Each path is the average of that pitcher's pitches of one type in the 2026 regular season (minimum 30 pitches). Trajectories are rebuilt from Statcast's constant-acceleration model: velocity and acceleration (vx0, vy0, vz0, ax, ay, az) are defined at y = 50 ft, then solved back to release (y = 60.5 ft minus extension) and forward to the front of the plate (y = 17/12 ft). Separation is y-matched — the distance in the x–z plane at the same distance from the plate — at the commit point (23.8 ft in front of the plate, about when a hitter has to decide) and again at the plate, in inches. Playback is time-synchronized at release, in 8× slow motion; the faster pitch reaches the plate first. Data: Baseball Savant Statcast via this repo's DuckDB. No club marks are used.
